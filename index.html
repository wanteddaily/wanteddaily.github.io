About

Wanted Daily runs the YouTube channels Wanted Daily Clips and Wanted Daily Fails and the Instagram account @wanteddaily.clips. It's a fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

"Wanted Daily Uploader" is a private, internal tool. It runs on one computer operated by Wanted Daily and uploads the clips we make to our own YouTube channels and Instagram account, using the official YouTube Data API and Instagram API. Only Wanted Daily uses it. It does not let anyone else sign in or post, and it is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is the operator of Wanted Daily, based in Estonia (European Union). Contact: wanteddailyclips@gmail.com. This policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Estonian Personal Data Protection Act (isikuandmete kaitse seadus).

1. Our own accounts (YouTube and Instagram APIs)

When we sign in with our own Google account, the tool requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read our channel's basic details and our videos' view counts (youtube.readonly). On Instagram it publishes Reels to our own account and reads their view counts. It stores the login tokens, the IDs and links of videos we posted, and their view counts. These are kept on our own computer and only the account running the tool can read them. Video IDs, links and view counts received from the APIs are deleted after 30 days at the latest. If access is revoked, the tool stops working and we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in Instagram → Settings → Website permissions.

2. Public live streams

To find highlights, the tool temporarily records public GTA 6 live streams and their public chat (chat usernames and messages). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in making highlight clips (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Raw recordings are deleted after about 36 hours and chat logs within 3 days. Chat is only counted (message and emote rate) and read by software running on our own computer to judge whether a moment is a highlight; it is never published and never used to identify or profile anyone. Short edited clips and their speech transcripts are deleted from our computer after 14 days; published clips stay on YouTube and Instagram, each with the streamer's channel name and link.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share personal data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties, except to YouTube and Instagram when we publish our own clips. All processing runs on our own computer; no cloud AI service receives chat or video.

Cookies

Neither the tool nor this website sets cookies or stores anything on visitors' devices. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, www.aki.ee) or to the data protection authority in your country.
