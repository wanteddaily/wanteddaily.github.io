About

Wanted Daily runs the YouTube channels Wanted Daily Clips and Wanted Daily Fails and the Instagram account @wanteddailyclips. It's a fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

"Wanted Daily Uploader" is our own internal tool. It runs on one private computer and uploads the clips we edit to our own YouTube channels and Instagram account through the official YouTube Data API and Instagram API. The only people who use it are the people who run these channels. It doesn't post for anyone else and doesn't offer accounts to the public.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What the tool accesses. When we sign in to our own Google account, the tool asks for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information and video view counts (youtube.readonly). On Instagram it publishes Reels to our own account and reads their view counts.

What is stored. Login tokens, the IDs of videos we uploaded and their view counts are kept in a file and a local database on our own computer, readable only by the account that runs the tool. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising. No data about other YouTube or Instagram users is collected.

Use of Google user data. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API data is handled in line with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion. View counts are kept for up to 30 days, then deleted. Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram → Settings → Website permissions; we then delete the stored tokens. Email us to have any data deleted.

Terms of service

The tool is private software, used only by the operators of the channels listed above. It is provided as is, without warranty. Every clip credits and links the streamer it comes from. Streamers who want a clip removed can email us and we will take it down.
